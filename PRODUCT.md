# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: parents of kids in grades 3-8 in the Bay Area, deciding whether to sign their child up for a free coding/STEM class and wanting to trust who runs it. Secondary: the kids themselves, who see the site alongside their parent.

## Product Purpose
Hackathon4Kids runs free computer science and STEM classes, face-to-face and virtual, so younger kids build strong fundamentals. Success on the site: a parent joins the mailing list to hear about the next program or contest.

## Positioning
Free, and run entirely by high-school students who teach younger kids in their own community. Founded in 2018 by two high-school students. Donations go toward assisting underprivileged children.

## Operating Context
Programs run as seasonal bootcamps and contests (Summer 2021, Summer 2023, Winter 2024 coding contest on Code.org). Contest students sign in to Code.org with Google Classroom accounts; parents follow the instructions page at `/code.org`. Sign-up happens through a Google Form mailing list. Contact is by email.

## Product Principles
1. Parents first: answer "what will my kid learn, who teaches, what does it cost" fast.
2. Free and student-run is the story; say it plainly.
3. Every page leads to the mailing list.
4. Only true claims; leave room for real stats later.
